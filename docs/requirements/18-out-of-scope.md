# Out of Scope for Initial Release

## Do not build initially

- Native iPhone app
- Native Android app
- Fully automatic AI pricing
- Complex route optimization
- Custom accounting platform
- Hundreds of thin city pages
- Live GPS vehicle tracking
- Customer password management
- Advanced fleet telematics
- Automated credit underwriting
- Marketplace for third-party haulers
- Multi-company franchise support

## Why these are deferred

These features increase complexity without producing the fastest return during the first release.

The initial release should prioritize systems that:

- Generate bookings
- Protect the business
- Collect signed agreements
- Collect payments
- Prevent scheduling conflicts
- Reduce administrative work
- Improve customer communication

## Later evaluation criteria

A deferred feature should only be approved when:

- The business problem is documented.
- Existing tools cannot solve it adequately.
- Expected revenue or savings are measurable.
- Security and compliance risks are understood.
- The operational team is ready to use it.
