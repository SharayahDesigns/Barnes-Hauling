# Customer-Facing Content

## Required service information

The website should clearly explain:

- Services offered
- Items accepted
- Items not accepted
- Service areas
- Appointment windows
- Same-day availability
- Pricing approach
- Estimate process
- Payment methods
- Cancellation policy
- Weather policy
- Donation and recycling practices

## Dumpster content

- Dumpster sizes
- What fits in each size
- Dimensions
- Weight limits
- Rental duration
- Included tonnage
- Additional-day pricing
- Overage pricing
- Accepted debris
- Prohibited materials
- Loading-height rules
- Placement requirements
- Access requirements
- Permit responsibilities
- Delivery process
- Pickup process
- Failed-delivery fees
- Blocked-pickup fees

## Conversion content

Every major page should include:

- Clear headline
- Short explanation
- Trust evidence
- Service benefits
- How it works
- Relevant FAQs
- Primary call to action
- Call and text options
- Service-area confirmation

## Trust signals

Use real and verifiable information only:

- Actual customer reviews
- Real project photos
- Insurance information when confirmed
- Business licenses when applicable
- Years in business when confirmed
- Donation or recycling partners when confirmed
- Employee and equipment photos
- Clear contact information

## Local SEO

Create useful pages for genuine service areas. Avoid thin pages that only swap city names.

Each service-area page should include:

- Services available
- Local scheduling information
- Relevant disposal or access considerations
- Nearby areas served
- Original frequently asked questions
- Local contact and quote action
