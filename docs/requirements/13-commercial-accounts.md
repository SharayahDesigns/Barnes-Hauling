# Commercial Accounts

## Target customers

- Property managers
- Contractors
- Construction companies
- Retail locations
- Offices
- Apartment communities
- Real-estate professionals
- Restoration companies
- Schools
- Healthcare facilities
- Municipal organizations

## Commercial account features

- Company profile
- Multiple authorized users
- Multiple jobsite addresses
- Purchase-order numbers
- Recurring service schedules
- Preferred pricing
- Contract pricing
- Credit application
- Credit limits
- Net-15 or Net-30 terms
- Tax-exempt status
- Consolidated invoices
- Monthly statements
- Jobsite-specific contacts
- Downloadable service records
- Priority scheduling
- Account-specific notes

## Commercial booking

Commercial customers should be able to:

- Select a company and jobsite
- Enter PO or work-order number
- Choose approved service
- Request recurring service
- Attach site instructions
- Upload photos
- Assign onsite contact
- Download service confirmation

## Billing

Support:

- Card payment
- ACH later
- Invoice payment
- Deposits
- Credit terms
- Consolidated billing
- Account statements
- Past-due status
- Staff approval for credit changes

## Account permissions

Company administrators should control which employees can:

- Create bookings
- Approve quotes
- View invoices
- Pay balances
- Manage jobsites
- View all company activity
