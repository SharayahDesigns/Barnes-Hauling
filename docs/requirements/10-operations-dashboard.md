# Operations Dashboard

## Lead pipeline

Recommended stages:

```text
New lead
Estimate requested
Estimate in review
Estimate sent
Awaiting customer approval
Scheduled
In progress
Completed
Cancelled
Lost
Spam
```

## Dashboard modules

- Lead management
- Quote builder
- Booking calendar
- Dispatch board
- Crew assignments
- Vehicle management
- Dumpster inventory
- Customer records
- Signed documents
- Payments and refunds
- Weight tickets
- Overage review
- Notifications
- Commercial accounts
- Reporting
- Settings

## Crew mobile view

Employees should be able to:

- Start travel
- Notify customer that the crew is on the way
- Open navigation
- View job details
- View signed waivers
- Contact customer
- Take before photos
- Record final quote
- Obtain customer approval
- Add line items
- Record materials
- Take after photos
- Mark job complete
- Request or collect payment
- Record disposal fees
- Add internal notes

## Permissions

Recommended roles:

- Owner
- Administrator
- Dispatcher
- Estimator
- Crew lead
- Crew member
- Accounting
- Customer support
- Read-only analyst

Permissions should be granted by role and action, not by trusting editable user profile metadata.
