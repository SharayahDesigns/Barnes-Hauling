# Launch Success Metrics

## Lead generation

Track:

- Quote form starts
- Quote form completions
- Booking conversion rate
- Phone-call clicks
- Text-message clicks
- Service-area rejection rate
- Abandoned booking rate
- Source and campaign attribution

## Revenue

Track:

- Online booking revenue
- Average order value
- Deposit revenue
- Hauling revenue
- Dumpster-rental revenue
- Overage revenue
- Refund rate
- Failed-payment rate
- Discount usage

## Operations

Track:

- Jobs per crew per day
- On-time arrival rate
- Scheduling conflict rate
- Dumpster utilization
- Average rental length
- Late pickup rate
- Failed-delivery rate
- Unbilled overages
- Time from lead to booked job
- Time from completion to payment

## Customer experience

Track:

- Review request conversion
- Average review score
- Cancellation rate
- Rescheduling rate
- Support contact rate
- Repeat-customer rate
- Referral conversion
- SMS opt-out rate
- Customer portal usage

## Website quality

Targets:

- Lighthouse performance above 90
- Lighthouse accessibility above 90
- Lighthouse best practices above 90
- Lighthouse SEO above 90
- No critical accessibility violations
- No exposed secrets
- No public customer-document buckets
- No missing RLS policies
- No unverified payment webhooks

## Initial business targets

Final numeric targets should be set after Barnes provides current lead, booking, revenue, cancellation, and utilization baselines.
