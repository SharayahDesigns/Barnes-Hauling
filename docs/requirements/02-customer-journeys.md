# Customer Journeys

## Hauling and junk-removal journey

```text
Enter ZIP code or service address
    ↓
Confirm service availability
    ↓
Choose hauling service
    ↓
Select items or project type
    ↓
Upload photos
    ↓
Receive estimated price or range
    ↓
Choose appointment window
    ↓
Enter contact information
    ↓
Accept terms and communication consent
    ↓
Schedule estimate or pay reservation deposit
    ↓
Crew reviews job and confirms final quote
    ↓
Customer approves quote
    ↓
Customer signs waiver
    ↓
Crew completes work
    ↓
Customer pays remaining balance
    ↓
Receipt and review request are sent
```

## Dumpster-rental journey

```text
Enter delivery address
    ↓
Validate service area
    ↓
Select project and debris type
    ↓
Recommend dumpster size
    ↓
Choose rental duration
    ↓
Choose delivery date
    ↓
Choose placement location
    ↓
Display complete pricing
    ↓
Sign rental agreement and damage waiver
    ↓
Pay rental charge
    ↓
Authorize card for documented overages
    ↓
Schedule delivery
    ↓
Schedule or request pickup
    ↓
Record dump weight ticket
    ↓
Calculate and review overages
    ↓
Charge approved overages
    ↓
Send final itemized receipt
```

## Returning-customer journey

```text
Open secure customer portal link
    ↓
Review prior and active bookings
    ↓
Rebook previous service
    ↓
Update address or instructions
    ↓
Extend rental or request pickup
    ↓
Download signed documents and receipts
```

## Commercial-customer journey

```text
Create company account
    ↓
Add authorized users and jobsites
    ↓
Request preferred pricing
    ↓
Create service order with PO number
    ↓
Schedule recurring or one-time work
    ↓
Track service history
    ↓
Receive consolidated invoices
```

## Experience requirements

- The booking flow must work well on phones.
- Progress must be clearly shown.
- Customers must be able to save and resume complex quote requests.
- Pricing assumptions must be displayed before payment.
- Errors must explain how to fix the problem.
- Customers must receive immediate confirmation after every major action.
