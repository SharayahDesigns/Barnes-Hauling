- Dumpster booking flow
- Photo uploads
- Availability calendar
- Waiver signing
- Stripe checkout
- Email confirmations
- Basic SMS reminders

- Booking management

- Reviews
- Analytics

## Release gate

Do not launch until:

- Payment webhooks are tested.
- RLS policies are verified.
- Customer files are private.
- Waiver language has legal review.
- Booking conflicts are prevented.
- Cancellation and overage policies are published.
- Email and SMS opt-out behavior works.
- Mobile booking is tested.
